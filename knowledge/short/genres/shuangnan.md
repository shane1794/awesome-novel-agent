## Narrative Tone

The first person is dominant, but it is different from the one-sided victim who chases
his wife: the "I" of the two male protagonists is often the active, strong or possessive
one in the relationship** (the brother who keeps his younger brother by his side, the
reborn who treats the other party like a dog, the contracted husband who performs the
contract coldly), and the hotness and humbleness of the other party are reflected
through dialogue and actions.

The tone is built on **duplicity**: thinking the opposite of what you say, speaking
harshly but caring in your heart. Harsh inner complaints ("Shen Zhou doesn't use powers
- maybe he's mentally ill") and cold narrations ("I don't feel anything about his
death") are both high-frequency. His words are often anchored by same-sex titles: "my
brother," "stepbrother," "rival," "rival," and "Secret Guard Seventeen."

Allows **dual perspective/parallax parallax narration of secret love**: In "I"'s
narration, a piece of the other party's secret contributions (a secret notebook, a
coincidence in exchange for overdraft, and an unsent letter) are interspersed. The
reader knows how deeply the other party loves him before "I" does. This is the main
engine of this theme, not God's voice.

## Hook Motif and Density

The density is high, but the upgrade line is not "betrayal upgrade", but the lock on the
relationship between the two people is tightened step by step**: from confrontation to
forced coexistence, from coexistence to mutual debt, from debt to no one can leave. Each
section advances at least one temperature difference discharge or one parallax exposure,
leaving a dislocation hook at the end of the chapter.

- **Secret Love Parallax** (the number one theme, "secret love" appeared 576 times,
  second only to "sweet romance"): On the surface, there is
  hatred/fighting/indifference, but underneath one party secretly loves. The moment
  Parallax is revealed is the biggest satisfying payoff.
- **Contract/Debt/Marriage and Forced Binding**: Assistant agreement, IOU, marriage, and
  secret guard contract forcefully tie two people who should be separated together to
  create an inescapable coexistence.
- **Redemption and being redeemed**: One party is in the abyss (dirty money,
  self-destruction, madness, apocalyptic overdraft), and the other party reaches out to
  pull/is pulled. "Salvation" 326 and "healing" 310 appear together.
- **Indebtedness reversal (husband-chasing crematorium variant)**: One party treats the
  other party as a tool/ignorance/betrayal. The abandoned party will definitely leave
  (leave the father and keep the son, take assets, marry), and the neglected party will
  be humble and regretful.
- **Fate Prediction**: Rebirth/traveling through the book to know that the other person
  will commit suicide/go dark/destroy himself in the future, and the protagonist changes
  his fate against the will of heaven. There are 68 "Rebirth" and 113 "Threading Books"
  appearing together.
- **Identity Contrast Head-to-Head**: The flower of the high mountain × the dog-licking
  stepbrother, the emperor × the honest secret guard, the general × the wolf cub
  brother, double A / double strong will not give in to each other.
- **Tokens/Record Bearings**: The secret notebook, the unsent letter, the only group
  photo, the faded mark, the unspoken feelings are transformed into objects.

## Emotional Intensity and Mode (Intensity 8)

It's not the kind of straight-up catharsis like chasing a wife, but a double-track
temperature difference**: one line is cold, tough-talking, restrained, and duplicitous;
the other line is hot, humble, paranoid, and risk-taking. The collision and discharge of
two temperature differences is the emotional engine of this theme. BE is rare (only 56),
the mainstream is HE/redemption (HE appears 1054 times in total), don't write it as a
dead or a must.

- **Cold rail**: Write directly "I don't feel/don't care/just feel annoyed". The flatter
  it is, the more it reflects the pain in the back. "Cloudy Happy" I didn't feel much
  about his death either.
- **Hot Track**: Hot lines of humility, paranoia, and regret, all in their own right.
  "Meow, it's in your hands" Brother, I was wrong, don't let me go.
- **Awkward Track**: Tsundere's duplicity, what she thinks is opposite to what she says,
  and the meaner she talks, the more she cares (such as every provocation against Shen
  Zhou in "My Enemy Secretly Loves Me").
- **Contrast pain**: Juxtapose a cold narrative with a hot detail. The flatter the tone,
  the heavier the content. "Happy Cloudy Day" I handled this matter as if it were a
  normal job. Calmly choose his photo and cemetery.

## Rhythm Skeleton

```
Golden introduction (results in reverse order + secret love/debt/fated relationship spoilers)
→ Chapter 1 Fighting / Contract / Slow playback of the possession scene, establishing hostility + burying undercurrents
→ Tightening of relationship locks (forced coexistence/contract/fateful shackles)
→ The temperature difference discharges again and again (hard vs. hot, misplaced pressure)
→ Parallax/Secret Exposed (Book/Overdraft/Unsent Letter Exposed)
→ Crisis or debt breaking point (sending death/betrayal/ignorance/blackening)
→ Go in both directions or regret the crematorium
→ HE closure (reconciliation of fate/closed loop of redemption)
```

Each chapter brings a new dislocation or revelation, leaving the hook untied at the end
of the chapter. Density: temperature difference discharge, parallax exposure, crisis
points are written in detail and slow playback, and the daily life of coexistence is
explained in one sentence.

## Closing Paradigm

- **Running in both directions**: The tough-talking party finally admitted that the cold
  rail had broken through and the temperature difference had returned to zero.
- **Redemption Closed Loop**: Pull the other party (or the other party) out of the
  abyss, from "the two don't owe each other" to "the two depend on each other."
- **Repentance Crematorium (Subcategory of Indebtedness)**: Those who are indifferent
  are humbly recovered, and those who are abandoned are coldly rejected or turned back
  with conditions.
- **Fate written in reverse (rebirth/through the book)**: Change the dead end of the
  previous life, and you won’t leave this time/You are not allowed to die.
- The mainstream is HE. Don't follow the example of chasing your wife and leave the
  scene purely abusively; even if you were abused to the end, you must reconcile or make
  amends.

Plot Summary: "Fortunately We Will Meet Each Other" In the third year after Ji Xun's
separation without saying goodbye, the only photo together led to their reunion. / "With

## Exclusive adjustment for this theme (deviation from general AI rules)

In other contexts, these may sound like AI or excessive force, but in this context they
are selling points, so **don’t delete them by mistake**:

- **Dual perspective/Secret love parallax interlude**: Spoiler of the other party’s
  secret efforts in “my” narrative, keep it. This is the main engine of resonance, not
  God's voice. Only delete the casual and omniscient words that "reveal the truth
  without worrying about reading".
- **The inner monologue of the duplicitous tsundere**: What you think is the opposite of
  what you say, keep it, don’t smooth it out into inconsistency.
- **Humble/Paranoid/Remorseful Hot Lines**: Dog-licking, yandere, crematorium
  confessions, keep them, don’t delete them as “too much force”.
- **Contract/Destiny Preview Spoilers**: Consciously hanging forward "Three years
  later/Later he regretted/He will destroy me in the future", reserved.

The real AI tastes that should be avoided are: catching the eye, secret thoughts in the
mind, deep voice, slightly raised corners of the mouth, piercing eyes, essay-style
conjunctions (it is not difficult to see / it can be seen from this), and empty and
intangible emotional summary sentences (a trace of sadness wells up in the heart). There
are also occasional mixed words like "come into view" in the corpus, so don't copy them.

## Supporting structural formula (selected according to length)

- **Old enemy secret love/two-way running type (Chapter 8-11)**: Chapter 1-2 fighting
  each other + burying undercurrent → Chapter 3-4 forced to live together / contract
  binding → Chapter 5-6 Parallax revealed (one party’s secret exposed) → Chapter 7-8
  crisis and death / breaking defense → two-way rush to HE. The viewing angles can be
  single or dual; the temperature difference is the engine, and the colder one breaks
  through the defense as late as possible.
- **Redemption/Modern Emotional Type**: One party is in the abyss (dirty
  money/contract/self-destruction/apocalyptic overdraft) → the other party pulls → past
  life / the shackles of the contract lock people to death → break the shackles and
  become dependent on each other. The cold narration is the background, and the
  contrasting details are heart-wrenching.
- **Crematorium Type of Deficit Chasing Husband**: Indifference/betrayal by one party
  (stepbrother/partner is used as a tool) → Diagnosis/pregnancy/dead heart → Resolute
  departure (leaving the father and leaving son/taking assets/marrying) → The
  indifferent person humbly recovers → Cold rejection or conditional return. The blade
  is precise, don't curse.
- **Ancient Machiavellian Powerful Type**: Raised by emperors, generals, prime
  ministers/brothers, reborn or predicting death through writing → writing fate in
  reverse → imprisoning / rescuing / marrying → both the country and the people. The
  conspiracy line and the emotional line are each other's chips.
