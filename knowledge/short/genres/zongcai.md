don’t just copy Chasing Your Wife.

## Narrative Tone

The first person "I" the wealthy wife/fiancée dominates the whole process, with a sense
of closeness and heartbeat, just say it hurts. Start with a possessive title: "My
husband/my husband/my fiancé/Mrs. Gu", and embed wealthy family anchors (group,
shareholders' meeting, marriage, favor, Beijing circle, inheritance rights).

Dual-track switching, but the switching of this theme is more "cruel": the first part is
not only about venting grievances, but also often going **really crazy** (tearing in
public, online confrontation, smashing things); after the truth is revealed, it is cut
to **cold treatment**-not making trouble, saying "ok", watching a movie, and keeping
accounts. There is a lot of irony and ridicule in the cold treatment section.

When using plug-ins, there is an additional **complaint voice**: barrage/system/mind
voice/rebirth memory interjection, giving the protagonist a clear judgment and also
giving readers a satisfying payoff. This voice line is colloquial and expressive, and
can be either mean or refined.

It is allowed and encouraged to spoil the ending of "The Crematorium" in advance.

## Hook Motif and Density

Extremely high density: 3-5 betrayal incidents are packed into the introduction, at
least one new betrayal upgrade or a more cruel line in each section of the text, and
there must be a new explosive point at the end of the chapter. **Ladder Betrayal:
Morality → Economy → Life**, this theme especially likes to do enough "economy" (group,
equity, inheritance rights, clean up and leave the house).

- **Moral Kidnapping Chain** (Core Motif): The scumbag packages his betrayal as "You
  should be generous/She should be pitiful/You owe her/You should be grateful to me".
  The more confident and confident he is, the more hooked he is.
- **white moonlight / mistress trigger chain**: The husband has white moonlight / little
  green plum / daughter of a comrade / school girl, and sacrifices his wife for her at
  critical moments; white moonlight uses "delicacy and grievance" as a soft knife, often
  fainting in public / biting back at the sight of blood.
- **Slowly push forward the physical evidence to confirm the affair** (the signature of
  this subject, which is different from one-time eavesdropping): instead of smashing it
  all at once, rely on the accumulation of physical evidence one by one - dried flowers
  / reflection in the cup / perfume / a piece of hair / a trial outfit that should not
  be there - the reader accompanies the protagonist to verify step by step, a dull knife
  is more painful.
- **Moments/big screen text used as a knife**: sweet text + misplaced token (the watch
  given to her husband by the protagonist is worn on the white moonlight wrist/the two
  were on the big screen at the concert). The more affectionate the text, the duller the
  knife and the more painful it will be.
- **Ask for help during a disaster and you will be humiliated**: If you ask for help
  during a fire/cerebral hemorrhage/lost father, you should be replaced by indifference
  or "I am in a meeting" and "Family members please stay away".
- **Number/Years/The burden of gratitude is cleared again**: Five years of marriage,
  eight years of being together, a three-year agreement, kidnapping with
  kindness,heaping up the investment and then clearing it all in one sentence.
- **Plugins give reasons to wake up**: barrage/system/rebirth/voice in the head, giving
  the protagonist a basis for "not licking this time" and also for making complaints.
- **Crematorium Preview**: A spoiler payoff is inserted at the end of the introduction
  or from the male perspective ("He broke open my apartment late one night like crazy").

## Emotional Intensity and Mode (Intensity 9, Dual Level: Crazy → Cold Treatment)

There are two clear stages of the heart-death switch in this theme. The intensity does
not decrease throughout the process, only the form changes.

- **Previous part (madness/catharsis)**: Name the emotion directly and intensify it,
  tears are disappointing, your heart is as gray as death, and your whole body feels
  cold; you can tear up in public, fight online, and smash things. Directly written
  emotions + physical reactions ** superimposed ** (nails pinching palms / almost
  nauseating / chills down the spine).
- **The second part (cold treatment/trial)**: After the truth is revealed, it is cool.
  Don't make trouble, just say hello, and watch the show. The emotion doesn't subside
  and turns into "coldness" - sarcasm, complaints, and accounting. The most devastating
  contrast is that the heart turns cold after death: "These words can no longer stir up
  any waves in me."
- The sobriety brought by the plug-in makes the latter part cold and reasonable, not
  abrupt.

Real example:

- "Wen Sheng Has Two Ideas" My tears fell down unsatisfactoryly. I didn't know whether
  it was because of grievance or pain.
- "Bai Xu Kong Nuo Ai Cheng Fo" I felt chills running down my spine, I pinched my palms
  with my nails to suppress the trembling all over, and dialed the number without any
  notes.
- "Five Years of Love, Two Clears in One Day" From that day on, my heart didn't seem to
  hurt so much anymore. But somewhere in my heart, it became completely quiet.
- "My Boyfriend Is a PUA Master" I went from being crazy at the beginning to turning a
  blind eye later, and even drove him to the hotel with other women.

## Rhythm Skeleton

```
Introduction to gold (reverse order of results + plug-in/identity card + crematorium preview)
→ Chapter 1 rewrites the introductory events, completes the moral kidnapping dialogue and physical experience
→ Slowly push forward the physical evidence/disaster rescue and confirm the derailment step by step (moral → economic upgrade)
→ Eavesdropping/With complete physical evidence, the whole truth is revealed
→ The heart is frozen (switch from crazy to cold treatment)
→ Cold treatment trial + Clean up and leave home/Economic countermeasures/Reveal your identity
→ The scumbag regrets and goes crazy (Crematorium)
```

Each chapter contains a new information bomb, leaving a hook at the end of the chapter
to keep the mood at bay. Density: physical evidence verification/moral
kidnapping/reversal in public is written in detail and played slowly, and the cutscene
is passed in one sentence.

## Exclusive adjustment for this theme (deviation from general AI rules)

These are like AI flavor or deletable redundancy in other contexts, but they are a
selling point in this subject, **don’t delete them by mistake**:

- **Moral kidnapping quotes**: The more outrageous and outrageous the logic, the more
  correct it is ("She has depression, you have to be generous", "I cheated because of
  your workaholic"). Don't change the order, don't add narration to break the story for
  the readers - what the readers want is to see the scumbag say it himself.
- **Physical Evidence Detective-style Inner Reasoning Segment**: Monologues such as
  "self-proof and not overthinking" such as enlarging photos and checking whether the
  brand has any free promotions are slow-moving engines. Don't treat them as verbose
  deletions; the knife will be blunt after deletion.
- **Barrage/System/Voices and complaints**: Internet sense, spoken language, can be
  cheap ("It is better to have a long mouth than not to have a long mouth", "Six nuclear
  bombs"), don't elegance, don't unify it into written language.
- **Puxin's scumbag's words and repetitions** ("I only love you, Ning Ning loves me and
  I won't marry her" repeated): This is deliberate, reflecting the dullness of PUA and
  the protagonist's sense of powerlessness. Don't treat it as redundant repetition.
- **Direct emotional idioms** (Heart as gray/Cold as ice): Keep it, just match it with
  an action or object unique to the scene, don’t replace it with pure action.
- **Crematorium Preview/Reverse Result Synopsis**: Keep this kind of conscious forward
  spoiler; only delete the casual God-like accent that "reveals suspense without
  worrying about reading".
- **Judgement Sentences/Heart-Death Ending Chapter**: Keep; only delete empty
  philosophical sublimation.

The real AI tastes that should be avoided are: catching the eye, secret thoughts in the
heart, deep voice, slightly raised corners of the mouth, piercing eyes, essay-style
conjunctions (it is not difficult to see / it can be seen from this), and empty and
intangible emotional summary sentences (a trace of sadness wells up in the heart).

## Supporting structural formula (selected according to length)

- **Confirmation of cheating/unforgiveness (Chapter 8-11)**: Chapters 1-2, slow
  introduction of physical evidence + moral kidnapping (flower/cup shadow/perfume/hair,
  one by one) → Chapter 3-4, asking for help in disaster and being humiliated + White
  Moonlight bites back in public → Chapter 5, complete truth and determination → Chapter
  6, switch and cold treatment → Chapter 7-8, cleansing and leaving the house +
  economic/identity countermeasures → The scumbag regrets being rejected. 100%
  first-person; the moral kidnapping dialogue is "reasonable" and not abusive; the
  awakening can be instantaneous (crash) or triggered by a plug-in; it is fun to release
  in sequence.
- **Cut-in sobriety type (barrage / system / rebirth) **: first explain the death method
  / system task / rebirth node in the previous life → the plug-in is online for sobriety
  and complaints → take the initiative to do "things that you would never do in the
  previous life" (voluntarily give away the group / white moonlight / status, or
  resolutely blacklist and leave) → the scumbag goes from being ordinary to crazy and
  regretful. The external voice line makes fun of the whole process.
- **Wealthy Equity/Identity Counterattack Type**: Starting shareholders
  meeting/marriage/gratitude kidnapping → the scumbag man brings his illegitimate
  daughter / white moonlight seizes the property → the protagonist has long been out of
  pocket / holding the trump card / true identity reversal → overturning the situation
  in public → leaving the game indifferently.
