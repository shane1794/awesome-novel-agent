# Western Fantasy Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable themes: western-fantasy

## High-Frequency AI-Style Bad Sentences: Incorrect vs. Correct Examples

### 1. Stacking of magic descriptions

❌ "He recited the incantation, and a blazing fire ignited in his hands" ✅ "He spread his
hands. The palms of his hands were itchy, like being bitten by mosquitoes. He closed his
fingers and recited two words. Nothing happened. He recited it again."

### 2. Formulaic racial depictions

❌ "The elf has pointed ears, long silver hair, and a noble and elegant temperament" ✅
"Her ears were pointed. He didn't know it when he was a child, but she pulled her. Now
he knows that it is natural, and he is used to it."

### 3. Direct emotional statements

❌ "A sense of mission surged in his heart" ✅ "He picked up the sword from the ground.
The sword was heavier than expected. He changed his hand, and the pattern carved on the
hilt hurt his hand. He used his sleeves to pad it and carried it on his shoulder."

### 4. Comparative analysis

❌ "Maybe it's a problem with the dragon, maybe it's a problem with the castle, maybe
it's a problem with myself" ✅ "He was sitting on the steps. The steps were cold, his
pants were thin, and he felt very uncomfortable. He wanted to change places, but he was
too lazy to move."

### 5. Over-description of the scene

❌ "The castle stood majestically, with its spire reaching into the sky" ✅ "There were
cracks in the wall of the castle. He counted them as he passed by, and there were seven.
The longest one could fit two fingers in."

## Writing points

1. **Magic writing failed** - It's not "successful casting", it's just no response after
   reciting it twice, palms are itchy, and hands are shaking.
2. **Ethnicity Writes Everyday Life**, Pointed ears, longevity, magic, brought through
   with specific life details
3. **The mission is written heavily**, Carrying the sword, changing hands, padding
   sleeves, the sense of mission is reflected in actions rather than in the heart
4. **Fantasy writing is dilapidated** - The castle has cracks, the sword hilt hurts the
   hand, and the magic sometimes works.
5. **Culture is specific**, Western fantasy is not the "Middle Ages", it is specific
   food, clothes, and customs
