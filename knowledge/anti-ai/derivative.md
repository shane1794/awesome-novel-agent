# Male-Oriented Derived Anti-AI Rules

> Applicable themes: derivative, male-derivative

## High-Frequency AI-Style Bad Sentences: Incorrect vs. Correct Examples

### 1. Description of counterattack

❌ "He didn't expect that he would have this day, and everyone was looking at him" ✅ "He
still walked with his head down. His phone rang, and it was a message. He took a look,
locked the screen, and kept walking."

### 2. face-slapping Description

❌ "Everyone was shocked, they didn't expect the waste to have such strength" ✅ "He put
down the things in his hands. No one spoke. He waited, but no one spoke. Then someone
coughed, and he walked forward."

### 3. Parallel shock

❌ "Maybe he was faking it, maybe he went through the back door, maybe it was all an
illusion" ✅ "He adjusted his glasses. He adjusted them again. There was no dust on the
lenses. He didn't know where to look."

### 4. Direct emotional statements

❌ "He felt more satisfied than ever before" ✅ "He leaned on the back of the chair and
put his feet up on the table. The water in the cup swayed but did not spill. He smiled
but did not make a sound."

### 5. Pretentious description

❌ "He smiled slightly, hiding his merit and fame" ✅ "He turned around and walked away.
He took two steps and put his hands in his pockets. His coat was too long and hung
behind his back, and he ignored it."

## Writing points

1. **Counter attack and daily writing**, What will you do after success? It’s not a
   celebration, it’s just continuing the old habits
2. **face-slapping means silence**, It’s not that “everyone was shocked”, it’s that no
   one spoke or waited for two seconds.
3. **Pretend to write small gestures** - It's not "a slight smile", it's just putting
   your hands in your pockets, hanging your coat down, and being too lazy to care.
4. **Satisfaction is about relaxation**, Satisfaction is not about laughing out loud, it
   is about putting your feet up, rocking your chair, and doing nothing.
5. **The tempo should be fast**, The male-oriented derivative has a fast tempo, don’t
   drag the face-slapping
