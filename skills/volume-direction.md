
# Volume Direction Skill

## Process

First time: present the story structure templates, and the author confirms the skeleton
Volume N+1: characters speak, then the 4-step check, then the volume direction is set

## 1. Volume N+1: Let the Characters Speak

When there is an archive from a previous volume (`archives/` is not empty), **start directly from character-driven analysis**, without pushing templates.

1. Read the unresolved hooks from the previous volume and the protagonist's current state
2. Read the latest state history of each active character
3. For each active character (including the antagonist and important supporting characters), generate a character "voice":

```text
[{Character Name}'s Situation] Where the volume ended: who they are with, what information they hold, what situation they are in
[{Character Name}'s Unfinished Business] The obsession, goal, or fear left over from the previous volume: what is still unresolved
[{Character Name}'s Between-Volume Thoughts] What they keep turning over in the gap between volumes
[{Character Name}'s Next Move] Based on the current situation, what they are most likely to push for in the next volume
```

4. Show it to the author. The clash of several characters' wills will naturally form the main line.
5. **From voices to chapter development (4-step check):**
   - Check whether the main line has emerged: do all characters' "next moves" point at the same contradiction?
   - Derive the chapter count: roughly how many chapters does each character's "next move" need to complete? Add them up to get the volume length
   - Check the chapter outline direction: can the main line be broken into stage-by-stage directions?
   - Templates are optional: when the main line is clear, a template is an auxiliary skeleton
6. The author sets the volume direction based on the character voices plus the 4-step check results.

**Fallback:** If the author still has no direction after the character voices, return to the templates to find structure.

## 2. Volume 1: Present the Story Structure Templates

When planning the volume outline for the first time (`archives/` is empty), use the structure templates to provide a skeleton.

The agent outputs a proposal directly (without asking "which one do you want"), guessing the most suitable template from the genre:

| Template | Suits | Default Volume Split |
|------|------|---------|
| **Three-Act** (setup → confrontation → resolution) | Conflict-driven, with a clear opponent or goal | setup 3-4 chapters / confrontation 4-5 chapters / resolution 2-3 chapters |
| **Kishotenketsu** (introduction → development → twist → conclusion) | Eastern narrative structure, slow and delicate | introduction 2-3 chapters / development 3-4 chapters / twist 2-3 chapters / conclusion 1-2 chapters |
| **Escalating Mystery** (question → clues → reversal → answer → aftertaste) | Puzzle-driven, with an information gap | question 1-2 chapters / clues 2-3 chapters / reversal 2 chapters / answer 1 chapter / aftertaste 1 chapter |
| **Character Arc** (everyday life → turning point → struggle → transformation → new normal) | Character-driven, growth-oriented | everyday life 2 chapters / turning point 2 chapters / struggle 2-3 chapters / transformation 1-2 chapters / new normal 1 chapter |
